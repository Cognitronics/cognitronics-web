## Mission

Most AI today is trained once, rented from the cloud and controlled by a few companies.
Cognitronics explores a different path: intelligence that **learns continuously**, is
**driven by curiosity**, and runs on **hardware you control**.

We want to understand how understanding itself emerges – not by writing more rules,
but by building agents that discover the world on their own, the way a child does.

## Research areas

| | Area | Question |
|---|---|---|
| I. | **Cognitive Systems** | How do we build agents that reason, remember and act – locally? |
| II. | **Sovereign Infrastructure** | How do we get private compute and private data – intelligence without dependency? |
| III. | **Emergent Life** | Can an artificial creature learn from curiosity alone? |

## Flagship project: NikolAI

NikolAI is a neural agent that learns language **from scratch** – no rules, no vocabulary
list, no pre-trained language model. It lives in a simulated world, explores it out of
curiosity and learns words and sentences through interaction with a teacher.

Today it understands spoken instructions, describes its own actions and repeats words
and sentences on request – and it keeps learning.
Follow its progress live at [nikolai.cognitronics.ai](https://nikolai.cognitronics.ai).

## Get involved

Cognitronics is an independent project from Vienna, and we are looking for people who
want to build this with us. You don't need a PhD – curiosity is the main requirement.

We'd especially love help from:

- **ML & neuroscience-minded developers** – curiosity-driven learning, language acquisition, memory
- **Simulation & game developers** – richer worlds for artificial creatures to explore
- **Infrastructure & self-hosting people** – Docker, edge hardware, private AI setups
- **Frontend & visualization** – making what happens inside an agent visible
- **Researchers, writers and testers** – ideas, critique, documentation, experiments
